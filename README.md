# Simple Invoice Manager

A lightweight, intuitive, and efficient application designed to streamline the process of creating, managing, and tracking invoices. Perfect for freelancers, small business owners, and consultants who need a hassle-free way to bill clients and keep track of payments.

## 🚀 Features

- **Invoice Creation:** Quick and easy creation of professional invoices with customizable fields (Client details, Line items, Tax, Discounts).
- **Client Management:** Maintain a built-in directory of your clients for faster invoice generation.
- **Status Tracking:** Keep track of unpaid, paid, overdue, and draft invoices at a glance.
- **Export to PDF:** Download clean, well-formatted PDF invoices ready to send to clients.
- **Dashboard Overview:** View simple analytics like total revenue, pending balances, and recent invoice history.
